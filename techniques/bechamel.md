# Béchamel (White Sauce)

A classic French mother sauce made from butter, flour, and milk (or cream). Used as a base for lasagna layers, pasta bakes, and gratins.

## Basic method

1. Melt butter in a saucepan over low heat.
2. Add flour and stir vigorously to form a smooth paste (roux). Cook for 1–2 minutes to remove the raw flour taste.
3. Add the milk or cream gradually, whisking or stirring constantly to prevent lumps.
4. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring continuously, until the sauce thickens to the desired consistency.
5. Season with salt, white pepper, and optionally a pinch of nutmeg.

## Ratios

| Sauce thickness | Butter | Flour | Milk |
|----------------|--------|-------|------|
| Light (coating) | 15g | 15g | 500ml |
| Medium (layering) | 30g | 30g | 500ml |
| Thick (croquettes) | 60g | 60g | 500ml |
