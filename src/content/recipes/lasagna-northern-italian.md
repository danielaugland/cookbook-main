## Method

### Meat sauce

1. Remove the sausage from its casing and fry in a pan over medium heat, breaking it into crumbles, until cooked through. Set aside.
2. Poach the chicken breast in simmering water until no pink remains, then drain, cool slightly, and chop. Set aside.
3. Cook the onion in the olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the wine, water, tomato paste, and diced tomatoes and stir to combine. Add the celery, carrots, Italian seasoning, basil, and a grind of black pepper.
5. Add the cooked sausage and chicken. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 1 hour.

### White sauce

6. Melt the butter in a saucepan over low heat. Add the flour and stir well to form a paste. Gradually pour in the half-and-half, stirring constantly, and cook until the sauce thickens to a heavy-cream consistency. Season with the salt and a pinch of nutmeg. Remove from heat.

### Cheese mixture

7. Combine the mozzarella, Parmesan, and dried parsley in a bowl and set aside.

### Assembly

8. Heat oven to 180°C.
9. Partially cook the lasagna noodles according to the package directions — they should be pliable but not soft.
10. Spread 1 cup of meat sauce over the base of a 33 × 23 cm baking dish.
11. Layer noodles over the sauce. Spread half the white sauce over the noodles, then scatter half the cheese mixture on top.
12. Add another layer of noodles, then meat sauce, then the remaining white sauce, then the remaining meat sauce.
13. Top with the remaining cheese mixture.
14. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes, or until bubbling and golden on top.

## Notes

- The meat sauce benefits from a full hour of simmering — don't rush it.
- Partially cooking the noodles (not fully soft) prevents the layers from becoming mushy after baking.
